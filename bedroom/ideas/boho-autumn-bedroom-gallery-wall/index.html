Cosy Boho Bedroom with a DIY Gallery Wall for Autumn

A warm boho bedroom with a DIY gallery wall, trailing plants, rattan lighting and autumn-toned cushions. Easy seasonal updates on a budget.

When the evenings get darker, a bedroom should feel cosy and welcoming. This boho idea combines a DIY gallery wall, plenty of plants and warm autumn colours. Most of it can be done in an afternoon with things you may already own.

The look

Above the bed, a relaxed gallery wall mixes botanical prints, simple sketches and small frames. Trailing plants soften the edges, and a rattan pendant casts warm, patterned light in the evening.

Grey bedding keeps the base neutral, while burnt orange cushions bring in the colours of autumn leaves.

Why it works

  • Personal and affordable. A gallery wall can use postcards, prints and frames from thrift shops.
  • Seasonal flexibility. Change a few cushions and throws, and the room moves easily from summer to autumn.
  • Natural textures. Rattan, plants and knitted throws make the room feel warm and lived-in.

DIY: hang a gallery wall in four steps

  1. Gather your frames and lay them on the floor until the arrangement feels balanced.
  2. Trace each frame onto paper, cut it out and tape the shapes to the wall.
  3. Mark the nail positions through the paper, then remove it and hammer in the nails.
  4. Hang the largest piece first, then build out around it, keeping gaps of about 5 cm (2 inches).

Swap the orange accents for soft pinks in spring or crisp whites in summer, and the same room feels new all year.

Furniture & decor suggestions

  • A rattan or woven pendant light
  • Mixed frames for a gallery wall: prints, botanical art and small mirrors
  • Trailing plants such as pothos in hanging or wall planters
  • Burnt orange and rust cushions for autumn
  • A chunky knitted throw in grey or oatmeal

Organization tips

  1. Plan the gallery wall on the floor first, or trace frames on paper and tape them to the wall.
  2. Keep one shelf or planter for plants only, so greenery doesn't spread onto every surface.
  3. Store summer cushions and throws in a labelled box when you switch to autumn colours.
  4. Choose a bedside with a shelf to hold a basket for chargers and small items.
