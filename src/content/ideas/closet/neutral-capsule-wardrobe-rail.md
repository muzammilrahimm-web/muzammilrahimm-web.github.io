A capsule wardrobe is a small collection of clothes that all work together. Displayed on an **open rail**, it becomes part of your room's decor, and it's perfect for small homes without a built-in closet.

## The look

Soft **cream, camel and chocolate knitwear** hangs on simple wooden hangers. A tall bunch of **dried pampas grass** adds height and texture beside the rail, echoing the warm, natural colours of the clothes. It feels calm, cosy and perfectly suited to autumn and winter.

## Why it works

- **Less choice, less stress.** A smaller wardrobe makes getting dressed faster.
- **Space-saving.** A freestanding rail fits in a bedroom corner, hallway or studio flat.
- **Seasonal by design.** Only the current season is on display; the rest is stored away.

## How to build a seasonal capsule

1. **Take everything out** and pick your favourite pieces for the coming season.
2. **Choose a colour story**: for autumn, try cream, camel, brown and one deeper accent.
3. **Hang only what fits on the rail** with a little space between each hanger.
4. **Store the rest** in labelled fabric bags under the bed or on a high shelf.

When the season changes, swap the clothes and refresh the rail. It feels like a whole new wardrobe without buying anything new.
