If your drawers turn into a jumble every week, try **file folding**. Instead of stacking clothes in piles, you fold them into small rectangles and **stand them upright**. Every item is visible the moment you open the drawer.

## Why it works

- **You can see everything.** No more digging to the bottom of a pile for one T-shirt.
- **It saves space.** Upright folding often fits noticeably more into the same drawer.
- **It stays tidy.** Taking one item out doesn't collapse the rest.

## DIY: how to file-fold a T-shirt

1. **Lay the T-shirt flat**, face down, and smooth out any wrinkles.
2. **Fold each side in** towards the centre, sleeves included, to make a long rectangle.
3. **Fold it in half from the bottom**, then in half or thirds again, until it's small enough to stand on its edge.
4. **Stand it up** in the drawer with the folded edge facing up.

The same idea works for jeans, leggings, knitwear and even pyjamas. Thicker items just need fewer folds.

## Make it last

Add simple **drawer dividers** or small boxes to keep rows straight, and group items by type: T-shirts in one row, jeans in another. Once everything has a place, putting laundry away becomes much quicker.
