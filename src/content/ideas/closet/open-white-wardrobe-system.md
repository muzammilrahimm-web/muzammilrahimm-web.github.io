An open wardrobe system lets you see everything you own at a glance. With the right structure, it looks as calm and organized as a boutique display.

## The look

This **white modular system** combines adjustable shelves with a **hanging rail** for everyday pieces. Neutral clothing in cream, beige and white hangs on matching hangers. Boxes, a handbag and a woven basket sit neatly on the shelves above, each with plenty of space around it.

## Why it works

- **Visibility.** When you can see your clothes, you wear more of them.
- **Flexibility.** Shelves and rails can be moved as your wardrobe changes.
- **Calm through repetition.** Matching hangers and boxes make the system look tidy even when it's full.

## How to plan your own

1. **Measure your wall** and count how much hanging space you really need for long and short items.
2. **Split the system into zones**: hanging, folded clothes, shoes and accessories.
3. **Choose one material and colour** for boxes and baskets.
4. **Put daily items between hip and eye height**, where they're easiest to reach.

Because everything is on show, an open wardrobe works best when it's edited regularly. A quick tidy each week keeps it looking its best.
