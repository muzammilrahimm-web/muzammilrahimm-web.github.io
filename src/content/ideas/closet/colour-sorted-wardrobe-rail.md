This is one of the quickest ways to make any closet look more organized, and it costs nothing. Simply **arrange your hanging clothes by colour**, from light to dark.

## The look

Shirts and T-shirts move from **white through red, yellow and orange** to brown and black. The result is a gentle rainbow that looks tidy and cheerful, even in a very small closet.

## Why it works

- **Easy to find things.** You'll remember the colour of a top before anything else.
- **Shows what you own.** You'll quickly notice if you have far too many of one colour.
- **Looks calm.** Gradual colour changes feel orderly, even when the rail is full.

## How to colour-sort your wardrobe

1. **Take everything off the rail** and remove anything that needs washing, mending or donating.
2. **Switch to matching slim hangers** if you can. It instantly saves space.
3. **Sort into colour groups**: white, cream, pink, red, orange, yellow, green, blue, brown, grey and black.
4. **Hang light to dark**, left to right, keeping similar sleeve lengths together.

Five minutes of sorting can make a small closet feel completely new.
