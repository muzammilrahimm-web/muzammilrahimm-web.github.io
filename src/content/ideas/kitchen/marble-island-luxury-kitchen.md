A kitchen island is often the heart of a larger home: a place to cook, eat breakfast and talk with family. This design turns it into a true luxury feature with **marble, warm walnut and leather**.

## The look

The island is topped with a **white marble-effect worktop** that flows over the edge for a substantial, high-end look. On the seating side, **walnut panelling** adds warmth, paired with **three leather stools**. White shaker cabinets, a hexagonal tiled splashback and a stainless steel range hood complete the scene.

## The furniture arrangement

Good kitchens are planned around movement. Here, the island sits parallel to the main run of cabinets, creating a clear **work triangle** between the hob, sink and fridge. Seating faces into the kitchen, so the cook can still talk to anyone sitting at the island.

- **Prep side:** facing the hob, with drawers below for knives and tools.
- **Social side:** stools along the far edge, away from hot pans.
- **Clear paths:** enough space on every side for two people to pass.

## How to recreate it

1. **Measure carefully** before choosing an island. It needs generous walkways to feel luxurious rather than cramped.
2. **Choose one statement material**, such as marble, and keep everything else quieter.
3. **Add warmth** with wood and leather to balance cool stone and steel.
4. **Plan storage inside the island** so the worktop can stay beautifully clear.
