Spring is the perfect time to bring something fresh into the kitchen. A small **herb garden by the sink or window** adds colour, scent and flavour, and it takes just a few minutes to set up.

## The look

Fresh greens fill the corner beside a **brass tap**, set against a white worktop and soft greige cabinets. A bowl of apples adds a pop of red. It feels lively and natural, and it doesn't take any extra floor space, which makes it ideal for small kitchens.

## Why it works

- **Small-space friendly.** Herbs grow happily in a corner of a worktop or on a windowsill.
- **Useful and beautiful.** You cook with what you grow, so the display never feels like clutter.
- **Easy seasonal change.** Swap herbs for spring bulbs, summer flowers or winter citrus as the year moves on.

## DIY: start a kitchen herb garden

1. **Choose a bright spot**, ideally near a window that gets several hours of light.
2. **Pick three to five herbs** you use most, such as basil, parsley, chives, thyme and mint.
3. **Plant in pots with drainage holes** and stand them on a tray or saucers.
4. **Water little and often**, letting the top of the soil dry slightly between waterings.
5. **Trim regularly** to keep plants compact and full.

It's one of the easiest ways to make a small kitchen feel fresh for the new season.
