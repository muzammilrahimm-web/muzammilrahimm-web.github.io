Open shelving is one of the simplest ways to add storage and personality to a kitchen. In this bright white kitchen, **floating wooden shelves** hold everyday pots and pans, turning practical items into a cheerful display.

## The look

White walls and cabinets keep everything fresh and light. Warm **walnut shelves** stand out against the white, and a collection of **red cast-iron pots** adds a confident pop of colour. The worktops are almost completely clear, which makes the kitchen feel spacious and ready for cooking.

## Why it works

- **Affordable storage.** Floating shelves cost far less than new wall cabinets.
- **Everything within reach.** Items you use daily are always easy to grab.
- **A designed look.** A limited colour palette makes everyday objects look intentional.

## How to recreate it

1. **Choose a wall** near your cooking zone, ideally where you'd otherwise have empty space.
2. **Fit two or three shelves** with strong concealed brackets, fixed securely into studs or solid masonry. If in doubt, ask a professional.
3. **Edit what goes on them.** Pick one accent colour, like red, and keep the rest neutral.
4. **Keep the worktop clear** below, so the shelves remain the focal point.

The result is a kitchen that feels warm, personal and organized, without a full renovation.
