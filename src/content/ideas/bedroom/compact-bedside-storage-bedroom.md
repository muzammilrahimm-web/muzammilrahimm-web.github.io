Small bedrooms often struggle with one thing: there's nowhere to put the little things. This idea solves it with a few clever, affordable choices.

## The look

A clean grey-and-white scheme keeps the room bright, while **navy patterned cushions** give it character. The star of the room is a simple **bedside table with a drawer and an open shelf**. It's small enough for a narrow gap beside the bed but still holds everything you need at night.

A slim lamp with a metal arm takes up very little space, leaving the surface free for a glass of water or a book.

## Why it works

- **Hidden storage where you need it.** A drawer beside the bed stops small items from piling up.
- **Light colours.** White and soft grey reflect light, so the room feels bigger.
- **Personality through textiles.** Cushions and throws are an inexpensive way to add style, and easy to swap.

## How to recreate it

1. Measure the space beside your bed before buying a bedside table. Even 35–40 cm (about 14–16 inches) wide can work.
2. Choose a table with **both a drawer and a shelf**.
3. Pick one accent colour, such as navy, and repeat it in two or three cushions.
4. Use the space under the bed for anything you don't need every week.

It's a practical, budget-friendly way to make a compact bedroom calm and organized.
