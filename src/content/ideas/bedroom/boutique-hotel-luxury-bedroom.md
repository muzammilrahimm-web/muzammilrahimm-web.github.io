Some bedrooms are designed to feel like a retreat. This boutique-hotel style uses rich textures, a darker colour palette and statement lighting to create a sense of everyday luxury.

## The look

A **dark charcoal wall** behind the bed creates depth and drama. Against it, a **grey channel-tufted bed** in soft fabric becomes the focal point. Matching bedside tables and lamps on each side give the room a balanced, hotel-like symmetry.

Above, a **brass chandelier** adds warmth and sparkle, while long grey curtains frame the windows. Two fluffy stools at the foot of the bed add a playful, tactile finishing touch.

## Why it works

- **Contrast.** Light bedding against a dark wall feels crisp and intentional.
- **Symmetry.** Matching pairs on either side of the bed look calm and expensive.
- **Layered textures.** Velvet, faux fur and linen make the room inviting, not cold.

## How to recreate it

1. **Paint one feature wall** in a deep charcoal or slate behind the bed.
2. **Invest in the bed.** An upholstered frame is the piece that sets the tone.
3. **Add warm metallics** through one statement light fitting rather than many small accents.
4. **Finish with soft layers**: a throw, a few cushions and a textured rug.

Luxury doesn't mean clutter. Keep surfaces clear and storage hidden, and the room will feel like a hotel suite every night.
