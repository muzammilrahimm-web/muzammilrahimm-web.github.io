Working from home in a small apartment means finding space for a desk without losing your living room. This idea uses **colour, not walls**, to create a dedicated work corner.

## The look

One wall is painted a rich **sage green**, instantly marking it as the work zone. A slim wooden desk sits against it, with a few **pinned prints and notes** above for inspiration. Plants and a bright window keep the corner fresh, while a patterned rug ties it into the rest of the living room.

## Why sage green works

Sage is a soft, natural green that feels calm and focused, and it pairs beautifully with wood, white and black. It's bold enough to define a zone but gentle enough to sit comfortably in a living room.

## The furniture arrangement

- **Desk against the coloured wall**, so the zone feels intentional.
- **Chair facing the wall**, with your back to the main living space, which helps with focus.
- **Lighting from the side**, from a window or a desk lamp, to avoid screen glare.

## How to recreate it

1. **Choose a corner** that gets good daylight.
2. **Paint just that wall**, or a large rectangle behind the desk, in sage green.
3. **Pick a slim desk** and a chair that tucks away neatly.
4. **Add one storage box** for work items so the corner can be cleared every evening.
