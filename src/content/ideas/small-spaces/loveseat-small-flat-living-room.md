In a small flat, the right-sized sofa makes all the difference. A **two-seater loveseat** leaves room to move while still giving you a comfortable place to relax.

## The look

A **powder blue loveseat** sits centred on a plain white wall, under a single **framed landscape print**. Navy and mustard cushions pick up colours from the artwork. A slim **tripod floor lamp** fills one corner and a small metal side table the other, leaving the rest of the room clear.

## The furniture arrangement

- **Centre the sofa** on the longest wall to create a natural focal point.
- **Balance the corners**: a lamp on one side and a side table on the other.
- **Skip the big coffee table** if space is tight; a side table is enough.

This simple, symmetrical layout feels calm and makes the most of every centimetre.

## How to recreate it

1. **Choose a loveseat** with slim arms and visible legs.
2. **Pick one large print** and two cushion colours from it.
3. **Add a tripod lamp** for warm evening light without a bulky base.
4. **Keep surfaces minimal**: one plant, one book and one candle are plenty.

It's an affordable, timeless layout for any compact living room.
