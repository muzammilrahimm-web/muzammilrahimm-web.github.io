A small living room can feel calm and generous with the right choices. This **soft neutral nook** proves that a compact apartment doesn't need to feel crowded.

## The look

A **light grey sofa** with a knitted throw sits beneath two simple framed prints. In front, a **round wooden coffee table** keeps the walkway clear, while **wall lights** on either side free up precious floor space. Plants bring in life and colour without clutter.

## Why it works

- **Light, airy colours.** Grey, white and natural wood reflect light and feel open.
- **Raised furniture.** A sofa on legs shows more floor, making the room feel bigger.
- **Rounded shapes.** A circular table softens the space and is easier to move around.

## How to recreate it

1. **Measure your room** and choose a sofa that leaves at least 60 cm (about 24 inches) of walkway in front.
2. **Pick a round or oval coffee table** in a warm wood tone.
3. **Mount wall lights or plug-in sconces** instead of adding floor lamps.
4. **Hang art in pairs** at the same height, centred above the sofa.
5. **Finish with two or three plants** in different heights.

It's a simple, affordable formula that works in almost any small apartment.
