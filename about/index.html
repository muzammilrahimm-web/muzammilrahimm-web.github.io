About us

Small homes, beautifully organized

Nestora is an independent home decor and organization journal for people who want calm, comfortable homes, whatever their size.

Welcome to Nestora

A home should work for you, not the other way around

Many of us live in apartments, flats and starter homes where every square foot counts. Nestora brings together simple, practical ideas to help those spaces feel open, organized and personal.

We focus on ideas you can actually use: storage that fits real rooms, routines that take minutes rather than weekends, and decor that makes a small space feel like home. Our guides are written for readers in both the United States and the United Kingdom, so you'll see closets and wardrobes, cabinets and cupboards side by side.

Our purpose

Mission and vision

Our mission

Make organizing simple and achievable

To share clear, honest and practical home organization and decor ideas that help anyone create a calmer home, without needing a big budget, a big house or a big weekend.

Our vision

Every space can feel like home

We imagine a world where small spaces are celebrated, not apologised for: homes that are easy to live in, easy to keep tidy and full of personality.

Our focus

Made for small-space living

Small rooms need different ideas. Everything we share is chosen with compact homes in mind.

  • Edit first. Owning less is the easiest way to make a room feel bigger.
  • Go vertical. Walls, doors and the space under the bed are valuable storage.
  • Choose hard-working furniture. Pieces that store, fold or do two jobs.
  • Keep it calm. Soft colours, natural textures and clear surfaces.
  • Make it easy to maintain. Small daily habits beat big yearly clear-outs.
