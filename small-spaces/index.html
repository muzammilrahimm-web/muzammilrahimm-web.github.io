Simple ideas to try this week

  1. 01

    Choose multi-purpose furniture

    Ottomans with storage, extendable tables and sofa beds work twice as hard.

  2. 02

    Go vertical

    Tall shelves and wall-mounted storage free up valuable floor space.

  3. 03

    Use mirrors and light

    A large mirror opposite a window bounces light around and makes a room feel bigger.

  4. 04

    Keep a clear path

    Leave open walkways so a small room feels easy to move through.
